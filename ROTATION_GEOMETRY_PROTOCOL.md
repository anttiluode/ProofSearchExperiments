# Experiment 7g — exact degree-two port chart and continuation
Frozen before new generator or solver execution. 96 fresh cases 76000–76095: n=6,7,8 (32 each), alternating sparse one-chord (overdetermined by count) and n-4 chords (square by count), 16 each per size. Same mixed-seed coefficient/diagonal range, chain and disjoint two-port supports {1,n-1},{2,n}. For square masks use chords (1,3),(2,n), then (i,i+2) lexicographic not already present until n-4. Count is not proof of Jacobian rank or generic solvability. Diagonals not assumed distinct. Witness matrix inaccessible to solver; witness-derived scalar retained as in 7f.
For disjoint port vectors u,v, u^T v=0. Canon aligns u on e1; align v on e2 using rotations in {2,...,n}, assert support and orthogonality. Parameterize Q=(I_2 plus U) R_(1,n-1)(alpha) R_(2,n)(beta), U in SO(n-2). This covers all SO(n) port-mask feasible transforms (angles unrestricted, no reflection needed: after peeling tail rotations first two rows equal e1,e2 and determinant U=+1). Internal generator increments on U followed by the two tail rotations, analytic transported derivatives via conjugating generators; audit raw matrix derivatives centrally at aligned nonzero-angle state. Exact manifold reduction, not linear subspace approximation. Feasibility includes final ratio and required entries; all physical port coordinates fixed by every rotation. Numerical leak checked on every evaluated chart endpoint, not artificial intermediate gauge coordinates. Include all residual rows including redundant port zeros to avoid a hidden Gram speedup. Full trial metric evaluated once, not every intermediate rotation; chart path records actual cumulative base rotations plus two final angle rotations. Charge both tail rotations each trial, align/seed costs and all evaluated rotations.
