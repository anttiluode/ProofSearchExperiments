# TWC-derived operator bridge
Use the frozen macro library from the symbolic pilot. Check whether its learned conjugation identity matches multiplication of two conjugated operators, without hand-supplying a new identity. For 32 seeded integer symmetric 4x4 matrices A,B, use an exact rational internal orthogonal rotation R = diag(1, [[3/5,-4/5],[4/5,3/5]],1) fixing the two ports. Evaluate (R^T A R)(R^T B R) and R^T (AB) R by exact BigInt numerator comparison. Count dense matrix products (5 vs 3) and compare to a hand-fused control (same 3). The product AB need not be reciprocal, so this is not a WaveProgram or proof of TWC backend legality. Separately verify exact preservation of U = diag(0,1,1,0), q = diag(1,0,0,1), and port vectors; compare complex S11/S21 numerically at fixed normalized frequencies for the single symmetric A and its rotated realization. Report maximum error, not hardware measurements. This is a standalone TWC-derived mathematical bridge, not an actual run of TWC or a topology search. No new macro training on these cases.
