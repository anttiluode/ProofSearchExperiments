## Controls and policies
- BASE-512: exact frozen solveH, cumulative 512 rotations per issued start, original continuation; compare diagnostic replay against frozen data before holdout. Its original global cap is the cumulative final quota, not the new hard meter. Check reported caps.
- LOCAL-512: same LM, per-start independent rotation quotas including seed rotations, compile once; smooth-to-full trigger at local 256 rotations OR original loss/accept/trial/damping triggers. This same stage schedule is used by every following arm, regardless of leash or scheduler. Local rotations do not include compile. Seed/RNG stream shared and issued in increasing restart ID; no reseeding on resume. This is a new matched control, not exact equivalence to BASE.
- SHORT-384, LONG-1024: change only independent per-start rotation limit.
- UNLEASH: local 512, then extend up to 1024 if last completed trial accepted and the recent four-trial full-loss drop exceeds 1% (relative to full loss at beginning of that window); reevaluate before every extra trial. No guarantee that progress implies convergence.
- HALVE-LOSS: batches of two, probe both 8 trials at n=8, 6 at n=9/2M, 10 at n=9/4M (or until terminal); rank by current full loss, finish winner to 1024, discard other; issue next pair if budget remains.
- SUSPEND-LOSS, SUSPEND-PROGRESS: same two-state probes and 1024 cap; repeatedly allocate four completed trials to best live state, retain all other state including cache, damping, objective stage, accepted count, path and local rotations. Rank by current full loss (lower better) or recent four-trial log full-loss decrease per modeled trial work (higher better, loss tie-break, then lower restart ID). No future labels. If one state is terminal, continue the other. New pair only after both terminate. Score history uses full loss even during smooth objective. Floor 1e-30. Do not spend a full step if the hard meter interrupts it; consumed partial work stays charged. A success anywhere stops immediately. At most two live states. No additional policy tuning. No claim that racing fits two trial-10 probes under 2M at n=9.
